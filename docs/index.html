01. Core Architectural Commitment

Kanri is designed from the ground up as a private, offline-first personal financial manager. Unlike traditional financial applications that require account creation, maintain central database servers, and harvest user transaction data, Kanri operates strictly on your personal device.

Our Absolute Privacy Promise: We do not operate proprietary user servers. We do not store, view, sell, monetize, or transmit your transaction logs, account balances, merchant names, or financial habits. Your data belongs exclusively to you.

02. Data Stored on Your Device

All data created while using Kanri is stored locally in private application sandbox storage utilizing encrypted Room/SQLite databases:

  • Ledger Transactions: Amount, timestamp, merchant/payee title, category classification, notes, payment mode (UPI, Card, Cash, NetBanking).
  • Budgets & Goals: Monthly budget thresholds, category spending limits, and savings goals.
  • User Profile: Locally assigned display name, optional date of birth, and locally cached avatar image.
  • Application Preferences: Theme selection, biometric lock state, and category customization rules.

04. Google Drive & OAuth Scopes

Kanri offers an optional Cloud Backup mode. When enabled, your ledger is backed up into your personal Google Drive account.

The Restricted drive.file Scope

Kanri requests strictly the limited scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This scope provides minimal necessary access:

  • Exclusive File Access: Kanri can only see, read, and modify the specific encrypted backup file (kanri_backup.enc) created by Kanri itself.
  • Zero Access to Other Files: Kanri cannot access, read, view, list, or delete your photos, documents, spreadsheets, or any other files stored in your Google Drive.

Client-Side Encryption

Before any backup file is uploaded to your Google Drive, it is encrypted on your device using industry-standard client-side encryption. Even when stored on Google Drive, your financial transactions cannot be read without your application keys.

Disconnection & Revocation

You can disconnect your Google account anytime in Settings > Storage & Backup Mode, switch to Fully Offline mode, or revoke Kanri's access entirely via your Google Account Security Dashboard.

05. Third-Party SDKs & Telemetry

To ensure uncompromising user privacy, Kanri implements a strict zero-telemetry policy:

  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not integrate Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Flurry.
  • No Advertising SDKs: There are no ad networks, banner ads, tracking beacons, or Advertising ID collectors.
  • Local Crash Diagnostics: Any application error logs are written solely to internal device cache for the user's inspection and are never broadcast automatically over the network.

06. Data Ownership, Deletion & Export

You maintain complete ownership of your data at all times:

  • Instant Data Deletion: You can completely erase all transactions, budgets, custom categories, and cached profile information at any moment by navigating to Settings > Danger Zone > Reset All Data.
  • Local File Export: You can export your complete ledger at any time in open formats (Encrypted JSON backup, PDF statements, and CSV spreadsheets) to your phone's Downloads directory.

07. Children's Privacy

Kanri is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children. Because Kanri operates on-device without user account registration, no age verification is conducted on external servers.
