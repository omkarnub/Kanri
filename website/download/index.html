Installing on Android 13, 14, and 15

Because Kanri is an open-source APK distributed outside Google Play, modern Android versions safeguard Notification Listener permissions behind a security feature called Restricted settings.

  1. Download and install Kanri-v1.19.8-release.apk.
  2. Launch Kanri. When prompted for Notification Listener access, Android may show "Restricted setting".
  3. Open your phone's Settings > Apps > Kanri.
  4. Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  5. Select Allow restricted settings and authenticate with your fingerprint or device PIN.
  6. Return to Kanri and toggle notification capture on.
