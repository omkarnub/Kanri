6. 18+ Insights & Financial Health Score

Explore Kanri's analytical visualizations and the mathematical mechanics behind the 4-pillar 0–100 Financial Health Score.

1. The 18+ Visualizations

Rendered using hardware-accelerated Compose Skia Canvas and AGSL shaders:

  • GitHub-Style Heatmap: 7-row calendar grid tracking spending intensity across discrete levels with day labels.
  • 365-Day Spending Matrix: Full-year holistic spending density map.
  • Burn-Rate Projection Line: Extrapolates current spending velocity to forecast end-of-month bank balance.
  • Velocity vs. Calendar Pacing: Real-time comparison of budget consumption percentage versus days elapsed.
  • Month-over-Month Delta: Day-by-day comparison of current spending versus the exact same day of the prior month.
  • Category Concentration: Clean donut and breakdown rows highlighting top discretionary outflow sectors.

2. The 4-Pillar Financial Health Score (0–100)

The FinancialHealthCalculator.kt engine synthesizes four 25-point pillars into a single holistic health index:

Pillar 1: Savings Rate (25 Points)

Formula: (Total Income - Total Spends) / Total Income

  • Savings Rate ≥ 20%: Full 25 Points
  • Savings Rate < 20%: Scaled proportionally: (rate ÷ 0.20) × 25
  • Negative savings (spending exceeding income): 0 Points

Pillar 2: Budget Adherence (25 Points)

Formula: Total Spent / Allocated Monthly Budget

  • Adherence ≤ 90%: Full 25 Points (healthy buffer maintained)
  • Adherence 90% to 100%: Points scaled down from 25 to 15.
  • Adherence > 100% (Over budget): Decays linearly to 0 points at 120% budget consumption.

Pillar 3: Spend Velocity vs. Calendar (25 Points)

Formula: Budget Used Percentage ÷ Elapsed Calendar Percentage

  • Velocity Ratio ≤ 1.0: Full 25 Points (spending at or below natural day pace)
  • Velocity Ratio > 1.0: Scaled down proportionally based on premature budget exhaustion risk.

Pillar 4: Debt Ratio (25 Points)

Formula: Receivables (Lent) ÷ (Receivables + Payables)

  • Zero debt or net positive balance: Full 25 Points
  • Liabilities exceeding assets: Points deducted proportionally to outstanding liabilities.

3. Grading Bands

Grade Score Band Status Description
A+ 90 – 100 Optimal Sovereignty · Strong surplus, minimal debt, pace disciplined
A 75 – 89 Strong Health · Healthy savings rate, controlled spending
B 60 – 74 Balanced Pacing · Minor pacing deviations, stable baseline
C 40 – 59 Caution Advised · Budget near capacity, elevated liabilities
D 0 – 39 Critical Action · Negative savings rate, budget exhausted
