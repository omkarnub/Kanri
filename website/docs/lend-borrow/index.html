4. Lend & Borrow Hub & Bill Splitting

Keep track of personal debts, partial repayments, settlement reminders, and group bill splits with zero cloud servers.

1. Peer-to-Peer Debt Tracking

Kanri's LendingEntity.kt tracks money lent to or borrowed from contacts:

  • Receivables (Lent): Money someone owes you. Displayed in Mint Green (+₹) and counted as an asset in your Net Worth calculation.
  • Payables (Borrowed): Money you owe someone. Displayed in Coral Red (-₹) and counted as a liability.

2. Partial Repayments & Settlement History

Real-world loans are rarely repaid in a single lump sum. When a friend returns ₹500 out of a ₹2,000 loan:

  1. Kanri records a LendingRepaymentEntity timestamped event.
  2. The remaining balance is updated to ₹1,500.
  3. The ₹500 returned is credited directly into your Cash or Online wallet.
  4. A complete historical audit trail of every partial installment is preserved.

3. One-Tap WhatsApp Reminders & UPI Deep Links

Kanri eliminates awkward debt conversations by generating clean, polite payment reminders:

  • 1-Tap WhatsApp Reminder: Opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message to the contact: "Hey Rahul, a friendly reminder about the ₹1,500 balance for the weekend trip."
  • Prefilled UPI Deep Links: Kanri can generate standard upi://pay?pa=...&am=1500&tn=KanriSettlement URIs, allowing your friend to launch GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm with the amount and payee pre-filled.

4. Group Bill Splitting (2 to 20 People)

When you pay for a group dinner or shared cab:

  1. Enter the total amount paid (e.g. ₹1,950).
  2. Select the number of people sharing the bill (2 to 20).
  3. Choose Equal Split (e.g. ₹487.50 / person) or Custom Split with individual per-person itemization.
  4. Your own share is logged as an expense; the remaining portions can be transferred directly to the Lend & Borrow ledger under each contact's name with one tap.
