5. Salary-Synced Budgets & Savings Goals

Learn how Kanri breaks free from rigid calendar months with custom cycle start days, dynamic Safe-to-Spend allowances, and pacing-aware savings goals.

1. Custom Budget Cycles (1st to 28th)

Most people receive salary on the 25th, 30th, or 5th of the month. Standard finance apps arbitrarily reset on the 1st, forcing you to track two halves of different paychecks.

In Kanri, configure BudgetPreferences.budgetCycleStartDay (1 to 28). If you choose 25:

  • Your financial month runs from October 25 to November 24.
  • All spending metrics, pacing bars, and home screen widgets synchronize automatically to this window.

2. The Safe-to-Spend Dynamic Allowance

Rather than a static number, Kanri continuously computes your safe daily spending allowance: 

Daily Safe Allowance = (Total Monthly Budget - Total Spent to Date) / Days Remaining in Cycle

If you underspend on Monday, your daily allowance for the remaining days increases automatically. If you make a large payment on Tuesday, the daily allowance recalculates smoothly so you don't run dry before payday.

3. Savings Goals with Pacing & Quick Steppers

Create specific savings milestones (e.g. "Emergency Fund", "New Laptop", "Trip to Japan"):

  • Milestone Indicators: Visual checkpoints at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% completion.
  • Pacing Tracks: Shows whether your current accumulation speed is ahead of or behind your target date.
  • Quick Stepper Deposits: Deposit unspent cash into goals with one-tap steppers: +₹500, +₹1,000, +₹2,000, +₹5,000.
