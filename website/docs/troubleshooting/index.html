11. Troubleshooting & OEM Battery Killers

Solutions for background capture freezes, aggressive OEM process termination, notification listener rebinding, and permission issues.

1. OEM Aggressive Battery Killers

Certain Android skins—especially Xiaomi (MIUI / HyperOS), OnePlus / OPPO / Realme (OxygenOS / ColorOS), and Vivo (FuntouchOS)—aggressively kill background broadcast receivers and services to artificially prolong battery benchmarks.

If transactions are not being captured when your phone screen is off, apply the following device-specific fixes:

Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO (MIUI & HyperOS)

  1. Go to Settings > Apps > Manage Apps > Kanri.
  2. Enable Autostart.
  3. Tap Battery Saver and set it to No restrictions.
  4. Open the Recent Apps screen, long-press Kanri, and tap the Lock icon to pin it in RAM.

OnePlus / OPPO / Realme (OxygenOS & ColorOS)

  1. Go to Settings > Battery > More Settings > Optimize battery use.
  2. Find Kanri and select Don't optimize.
  3. Go to Settings > Apps > Auto-launch and toggle Kanri on.

Samsung (One UI)

  1. Go to Settings > Apps > Kanri > Battery.
  2. Choose Unrestricted.
  3. Ensure Kanri is not listed in Device Care > Battery > Background usage limits > Sleeping apps.

2. Notification Listener Rebinding (Watchdog)

Occasionally, Android's internal NotificationManager unbinds NotificationListenerService instances following heavy memory pressure.

Kanri includes a self-healing background worker: NotificationListenerWatchdogWorker.kt.

  • Every few hours, the watchdog checks if the notification service is actively bound to the system.
  • If Android has silently disconnected the service, the watchdog programmatically toggles the component state via PackageManager to trigger a clean rebinding without requiring a phone restart.

3. Unsupported Bank Messages & Format Changes

If a specific bank transaction is not detected:

  1. Check whether the SMS came from an official sender code (e.g. VK-HDFCBK) rather than a personal 10-digit phone number.
  2. Confirm whether the message is a promotional alert or a debit/credit notification.
  3. To request support for an unlisted bank or a newly formatted SMS, copy the message text, sanitize your personal account number and balance, and open a ticket on our GitHub Issue Tracker.
