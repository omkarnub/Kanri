3. Dual-Wallet Architecture & ATM Modes

Learn how Kanri maintains accurate net worth by separating physical cash from digital banking, and how ATM cash withdrawals are handled without double-counting expenses.

1. The Dual-Wallet Separation

Most digital expense trackers track only bank or card balances, assuming all transactions are electronic. In India and cash-prevalent economies, physical cash represents a significant volume of daily payments.

Kanri models two isolated ledgers in WalletEntity.kt:

  • Online Wallet: Represents bank accounts, UPI linked accounts, and debit/credit card balances. Spends are automatically deducted upon receiving bank SMS or UPI notifications.
  • Cash Wallet: Represents physical currency notes and coins. Spends are logged manually via the 1-tap quick entry sheet or widget action buttons.

Your total Net Worth equals: Online Balance + Cash Balance + Receivables (Lent) - Liabilities (Borrowed).

2. Smart ATM Cash Withdrawal Modes

When you withdraw ₹2,000 from an ATM, your bank sends a debit SMS: "A/C **9012 debited by Rs 2000.00 at SBI ATM".

Standard expense trackers incorrectly record this ₹2,000 as an expense. If you later spend ₹500 of that cash on dinner and log it, your monthly spending is artificially inflated to ₹2,500.

Kanri's AtmWithdrawalMode solves this mathematically:

  1. Automatic ATM Detection: The regex engine identifies the ATM keyword in the SMS.
  2. Internal Transfer Execution: Kanri debits ₹2,000 from your Online Wallet and simultaneously credits ₹2,000 to your Cash Wallet.
  3. Zero Expense Impact: The ATM transaction has zero effect on your monthly budget, spending pace, or expense charts.
  4. Accurate Cash Spend Tracking: When you spend the cash later, logging ₹500 debits only your Cash Wallet, ensuring every rupee is counted exactly once.

3. Indian Numbering System (Lakh & Crore Formatting)

Kanri's typography engine (CurrencyUtils.kt) natively supports the Indian numbering system:

  • ₹850 (Hundreds)
  • ₹12,450 (Thousands)
  • ₹1.25L / ₹1,25,000 (Lakhs)
  • ₹2.40Cr / ₹2,40,00,000 (Crores)

Dot-matrix widget numerals in the Ndot57 font are optically scaled to ensure currency symbols and commas align neatly on the grid without text clipping.
